About

Your Gateway to Biotechnology Careers

The Bversity Placement Fair 2025 is designed to connect biotechnology, life science, and bioinformatics students and graduates with real industry opportunities. Explore the biotechnology industry, discover internship opportunities, and understand what employers look for in candidates. This event is tailored to help you prepare, practice, and apply for biotech roles.

Through interactive sessions, mock interviews, and networking, you'll gain the skills and confidence to succeed in the biotech industry. Build an ATS-friendly resume, gain interview experience, apply to real opportunities, and earn a certificate of participation. This is your chance to take the first step towards a successful biotechnology career.
